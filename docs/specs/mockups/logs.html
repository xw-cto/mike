Mike · Logs tab · scenario selector over a full filter

Two layers over one log. The scenario row is the high-level selector: a named preset for a common diagnostic question, which fills the low-level filter and says in one line what it set. The filter row is full control: level and above, component, seat, actor, project, event, text, a time range, a limit. Editing the filter after picking a scenario turns the chip to "Custom"; a human can save the current filter as a new scenario (config, human only). The filter is in the URL, so a view is a link. Lines show time, level, component, seat, event and message; a line expands to its fields and links to its decision record and the thing it names. Tick boundaries are thin separators. Live follows the stream; Pause stops the scroll, not the stream.
